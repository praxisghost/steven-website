# Language Guide Quality Standard

A guide is complete only if it contains:

## Introduction

Minimum:
500 words

---

## Grammar

Minimum:
1000 words

---

## Vocabulary

Minimum:
100 terms

Preferred:
150–300 terms

---

## Resources

Minimum:
10 resources

Must include:

* dictionary
* grammar
* listening
* reading
* video

---

## SRS

Must include:

Front:
Target language

Back:
Translation

For every vocabulary item.

---

## Total Length

Target:

3000–6000 words

per guide.

---

## Navigation

Must be added to language guide navigation.

Must be searchable.

Must be linked from parent language page.
