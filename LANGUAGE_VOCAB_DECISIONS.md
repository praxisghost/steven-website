# LANGUAGE_VOCAB_DECISIONS.md

## Purpose

Permanent architectural decisions for vocabulary expansion and Anki export generation.

Claude must consult this file before making modifications.

---

## Decision 001

Vocabulary additions must be sourced from reliable educational resources.

Examples:

* Wiktionary
* Tatoeba
* Frequency dictionaries
* CEFR resources
* JLPT resources
* HSK resources
* TOPIK resources
* DELE resources
* official language-learning materials

Status: Accepted

---

## Decision 002

Existing vocabulary entries must never be modified unless a clear factual error exists.

Status: Accepted

---

## Decision 003

Existing vocabulary entries must never be duplicated.

Status: Accepted

---

## Decision 004

New vocabulary should prioritize high-frequency words.

Status: Accepted

---

## Decision 005

Vocabulary expansion should occur incrementally.

Small high-quality additions are preferred over massive low-quality additions.

Status: Accepted

---

## Decision 006

Every language guide should eventually have a downloadable UTF-8 vocabulary export.

Status: Accepted

---

## Decision 007

Exports should be compatible with Anki.

Status: Accepted

---

## Decision 008

CSV files should preserve all table columns.

Status: Accepted

---

## Decision 009

The export schema should match the table schema.

Status: Accepted

---

## Decision 010

If multiple vocabulary tables exist on a page:

Export all vocabulary into a single downloadable file.

Status: Accepted

---

## Decision 011

Vocabulary accuracy is more important than vocabulary quantity.

Status: Accepted
