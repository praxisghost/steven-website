# Chicago Citation Standard

Use Chicago Notes and Bibliography style.

## Footnotes

Insert footnotes immediately after the supported claim.

Example:

1. UNESCO, "Atlas of the World's Languages in Danger," accessed June 1, 2026, https://www.unesco.org/.

## Bibliography

Create or update a bibliography section at the bottom of the page when citations are added.

Example:

UNESCO.

"Atlas of the World's Languages in Danger."

Accessed June 1, 2026.

https://www.unesco.org/

## Rules

* Use real URLs.
* Use real titles.
* Use real authors when available.
* Use real publication dates when available.
* Never fabricate metadata.
* Never fabricate authors.
* Never fabricate publication dates.
* Never fabricate URLs.

## Bibliography Placement

Only content pages should contain bibliographies.

Navigation and hub pages must not contain bibliographies.
