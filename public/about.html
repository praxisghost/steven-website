About

A bit about who I am & how I ended up here.

I'm an only child who loves learning, teaching, enjoying life, & meeting new people. This is the part of the site where I try to sound interesting without sounding like a LinkedIn profile.

Education

I graduated in 2022 with a Bachelor of Arts in German Studies from Wheaton College Massachusetts.

Work

I currently teach at EF International Language Center in Boston, Massachusetts. Most of my favorite work moments involve someone realizing they actually can say the sentence they've been afraid of.

Programs & Grants

I'm both:

  • a Congress-Bundestag Youth Exchange 2016 alumnus
  • & a Fulbright Germany English Teaching Assistant grant recipient (2024–2025)

Places I've Lived

Hobbies

Family & Animals

I'm the son of a public librarian & have always loved learning — somewhat unsurprisingly. I love animals, & I've always had pets of some kind: hamsters, dogs, cats, lizards, & frogs. I especially love frogs.
