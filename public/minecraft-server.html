MEINKRAFT | Survival PvP | Red Blue Green Teams

Welcome to Stove's Meinkraft Factions — a hard-mode survival PvP server running Paper 26.1.2. Pick your team (Red, Blue, or Green), stake a claim, build up your base, and fight for your color. Vanilla at heart, spiced up with light server-side plugins: duels, jobs, quests, custom enchantments, blood moons, and proximity voice chat.

Server Address — click to copy

You must be running Vanilla Minecraft 26.1 / 26.1.2 (Java Edition). No mods or client setup needed — just add the address under Multiplayer → Add Server and join.

How It Plays

  • ⚔️ Three-Team PvPChoose Red, Blue, or Green when you join. Friendly fire is off within your team — everyone else is fair game.
  • 🌍 Hard-Mode SurvivalHard difficulty, survival gamemode, monsters on. Blood moons and infernal mobs make nights genuinely dangerous.
  • 🥊 DuelsSettle scores one-on-one with /duel — even against your own teammates.
  • 🏠 Land ClaimsProtect your base with the golden shovel. Grief protection and block logging keep your builds safe.
  • 💰 Jobs & EconomyEarn money through jobs, trade with player shops and sign shops, and complete quests for rewards.
  • ✨ Custom GearCustom enchantments, special items, guns via QualityArmory, and grappling around the map.
  • 🎙️ Proximity Voice ChatSimple Voice Chat is installed — optional, but talking to whoever's standing next to you is half the fun.
  • 🚄 Speedy MinecartsRail networks run up to 8× vanilla speed. Build the transcontinental railway of your dreams.
