Side projects and one-off pages that don't fit a blog post. They'll collect here as they're ready to share.

World History Timeline

An interactive timeline of world history I've been building event by event, using Knight Lab's TimelineJS. It runs from the first civilizations to the present — the Chinese dynasties, Greece and Rome, the medieval world, the empires and wars, and the histories that usually get left out of the big sweep. Each entry is checked against its own source, and the whole thing is still growing. It has nowhere to live publicly yet.

Guitar Site

A practice companion for guitar scales: every key across the common scale types, shown on a playable fretboard, with a note-and-melody generator so you can hear the pattern while you learn it. It exists locally for now — no public address yet.
