Political Opinion

My political opinions will live here. I will do my best to be critical of my own ideology & to challenge my own views harshly. In short - violence is always wrong - practical organizing, policy making, democracy, fighting class warfare, unionization, anti-trust regulation, anti-war, are the paths to individual & societal liberation. Only the people can bring an end to racial, gender, & sexual, or theological discrimination & we must do so by fighting for the end to this income inequality, end this oppression of the working class. Justice must be served for the wrongs of the ages of imperialism & colonialism. The world is not void of slavery therefore there is bitter work more important than anything that we must do. How do we make life better for our neighbors? Then can we maybe make life better for people further than us than that? We must do more. Will we let humanity fall to consumerism, capitalism, greed? We must decide. Our planet & humanity are at stake. More soon.
