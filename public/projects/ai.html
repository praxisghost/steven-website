AI

Experiments, tools, and thoughts at the intersection of language, learning, and machine intelligence.

AI interests me primarily through the lens of language learning and education — how it can help people learn faster, understand things more deeply, and access knowledge that was previously locked behind barriers of time, money, or expertise.

I'm also building this website with AI assistance, which has been its own education in prompting, context, and working with a very patient co-developer. Projects and write-ups in progress.
