Circuits

Hardware tinkering, hobby electronics, and the thrill of making something blink.

From Raspberry Pi setups to basic breadboard experiments, I enjoy hobby electronics as a way to bridge the gap between software and the physical world. There's something deeply satisfying about building a circuit that actually does what you intended — and equally educational when it doesn't.

Projects and notes coming here as things develop. Expect microcontrollers, sensors, maybe some cursing, and eventually something that works.
