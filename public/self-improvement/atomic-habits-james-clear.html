Atomic Habits

James Clear

The basic idea, very shortly: tiny habits compound. Don't obsess over goals — build systems, and let small, repeated actions reshape who you are. Real change isn't about outcomes; it's about becoming the kind of person those outcomes belong to.

It's got a great hook and genuinely nice visuals, and the whole thing is easy to move through. As George Carlin put it — "If it's help from a book someone else wrote, it's not self-help — it's just help!" By that measure, this is my favorite book in the 'help' genre so far.

Quotes I highlighted on my first read

"Behavior that is incongruent with oneself will not last."

"True behavior change is identity change."

"The goal is not to read a book, the goal is to become a reader."

"It's a two step process. 1: Decide the type of person you want to be. 2: Prove it to yourself with small wins."

"If you can get 1% better each day for one year, you'll end up 37 times better by the time you're done."

"You do not rise to the level of your goals, you fall to the level of your system."

"You need to be patient. Focus on your systems."
