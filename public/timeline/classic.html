About this collection and its sources

This collection awaits independent historical review. Dates follow the precision of their sources; BCE dates precede CE, with no year zero. “c.” means approximate. Every event links to Wikipedia. Dates were compared with Wikidata and exceptions checked against source text. Images link to their Wikimedia Commons file pages and verified public-domain or Creative Commons terms. TimelineJS by Knight Lab is distributed under MPL 2.0. Wikipedia is a starting point for further reading, not the final authority.
