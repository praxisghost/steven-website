About this collection and its sources

This collection awaits independent historical review. Dates follow the precision of their sources. BC dates precede AD, with no year zero, and “c.” means approximate. Dates and years in other calendars are converted from the Gregorian year, so a year marked ≈ can differ by one. The axis gives crowded periods more room and shortens long empty stretches, so equal distances do not mean equal time.

Every event links to Wikipedia, which is a starting point for further reading and not the final authority. Dates were compared with Wikidata, and exceptions were checked against source text. Images link to their Wikimedia Commons file pages and verified public-domain or Creative Commons terms. Background text comes from Wikipedia under CC BY-SA 4.0.
