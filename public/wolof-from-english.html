1. Absolute Beginners — Start Here

Jàngum Wolof Learning Wolof Start your Wolof learning journey here.

Wolof is the most widely spoken language in Senegal and serves as a major lingua franca throughout the country. While French is the official language of Senegal, Wolof dominates daily conversation, music, markets, media, and urban life.

Wolof grammar is significantly different from English and most European languages, but pronunciation is generally approachable for English speakers. Consistent listening and speaking practice are essential.

Core Principles

  • Study every day, even briefly.
  • Focus heavily on listening comprehension early.
  • Practice speaking immediately — Wolof is highly conversational.
  • Learn vocabulary through repetition and real-world usage.
  • Music and media immersion are extremely valuable for Wolof learners.

Writing System

Wolof is usually written using the Latin alphabet. You may also encounter Ajami Wolof written in Arabic script, especially in religious contexts.

2. Online Tools

  • Google Translate — limited Wolof support; useful for quick vocabulary checks.
  • Forvo Wolof Pronunciations — native speaker pronunciation recordings.
  • Anki — essential for vocabulary memorization.

3. Finding a Tutor

iTalki is currently the best platform for finding Wolof tutors from Senegal.

Because Wolof is less commonly taught than major world languages, tutor availability may fluctuate. Search using both “Wolof” and “Senegalese Wolof.”

  • Preply — alternative tutoring marketplace.

4. Books

  • Colloquial Wolof — practical beginner coursebook.
  • Peace Corps Wolof Manual — excellent free foundational material.
  • Wolof Dictionary & Phrasebook — useful for travelers and beginners.

5. Complete Courses

  • Peace Corps Wolof Course — one of the best free Wolof resources available online.
  • Memrise — community-created Wolof vocabulary decks.
  • YouTube Wolof Lessons — increasingly valuable due to limited formal course availability.

6. Spaced Repetition Systems

Anki

Anki is strongly recommended for Wolof because high-quality textbooks and graded readers remain limited.

Build custom decks using:

  • Essential greetings
  • Daily verbs
  • Family vocabulary
  • Market and food vocabulary
  • Audio from native speakers

7. Vocabulary Resources
