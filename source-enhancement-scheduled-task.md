# Scheduled Task: Content Source Enhancement

## Purpose

This task improves the factual reliability and academic quality of content pages by selectively adding high-quality citations where they genuinely strengthen important claims.

The goal is not to maximize citations.

The goal is to strategically improve trustworthiness while maintaining readability.

## Scope

Process only content pages.

Do NOT process:

* Home page
* Navigation pages
* Language hub pages
* Directory pages
* Category pages
* Sitemap pages
* Search pages
* Administrative pages

## Batch Size

Review:

* 3–10 pages per execution

Update progress tracking after completion.

## Core Principle

Sources should be rare, intentional, and valuable.

A citation should only be added if it clearly improves the page.

The website should remain easy and pleasant to read.

## Citation Budget

Per page:

* Usually 0–3 citations

For research-heavy pages:

* Maximum 6 citations

Per execution:

* Maximum 15 new citations

## Workflow

### Step 1

Review page content.

### Step 2

Identify factual claims that would materially benefit from verification.

### Step 3

Search for the strongest available source.

### Step 4

Apply source-selection-policy.md.

### Step 5

Add Chicago-style footnotes where appropriate.

### Step 6

Update bibliography if citations were added.

### Step 7

Generate audit report.

## Success Criteria

Success is NOT measured by citation count.

Success is measured by:

* Improved reliability
* Improved credibility
* Improved reader trust
* Minimal citation clutter
* High source quality
